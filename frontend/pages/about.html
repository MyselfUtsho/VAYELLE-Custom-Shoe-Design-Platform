VAYELLE — Match It. Vibe It.

Custom Shoe Design and AI-Powered Recommendation Platform created for Team 03, Lab Section 01, Fall 2026.

Platform Concept & Mission

VAYELLE bridges personal self-expression, e-commerce, and creative technology. Instead of settling for off-the-shelf footwear mass-produced without personality, VAYELLE gives users interactive 2D and 3D customizer tools, intelligent outfit harmony calculations, and an open community remix culture.

Whether discovering silhouette designs, exploring color wheel harmonies, calculating costs against strict budget limits, or feeding an outfit snapshot to AI color extractors, VAYELLE turns shoe shoppers into fashion creators.
