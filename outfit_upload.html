📸 Tips for Best Results

☀️
Natural Lighting
Shoot near a window or outdoors. Avoid harsh shadows or dim indoor lights.
👕
Full Outfit Visible
Capture the whole look — top, bottom, and accessories for precise color analysis.
🎨
No Heavy Filters
Raw or lightly edited photos give the AI accurate color data. Avoid B&W or oversaturated filters.
🧱
Clean Background
A plain wall or neutral background helps isolate outfit colors for sharper recommendations.
👟
Include Current Shoes
Wearing shoes in the photo lets the AI understand your existing style baseline.
📐
Portrait Orientation
Vertical shots capture more of the outfit. Landscape crops may miss key styling details.
