📐 How to Measure

  1. 1
    Place a blank sheet of paper on a hard floor against a wall.
  2. 2
    Stand with your heel flat against the wall on the paper.
  3. 3
    Mark the longest point of your foot (usually the big toe) with a pencil.
  4. 4
    Measure from the wall edge to the mark in centimetres.
  5. 5
    Measure both feet and use the larger measurement.
  6. 6
    Measure in the afternoon — feet are slightly larger later in the day.
