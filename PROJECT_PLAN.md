## 1. Project Overview
VAYELLE is a web-based shoe customization and recommendation platform[cite: 13, 16]. Users can browse shoes, explore colors, upload outfit images, customize shoes through a 2D design canvas, estimate the price, receive AI-assisted recommendations, preview designs, save them, and share or remix community designs[cite: 13, 16].

- **Module 1 — Lab 5:** Shoe Discovery and Selection[cite: 16]
- **Module 2 — Lab 6:** Shoe Configuration and Analysis[cite: 16]
- **Module 3 — Lab 7:** Design Finalization, Community and Checkout[cite: 16]
